# Magnitai commercial product standard

Last reviewed: 29 July 2026

## The product we are selling

Magnitai is an **interactive lead magnet builder for existing websites**.

The positioning hierarchy is fixed:

1. **Customer outcome:** turn more existing website visitors into qualified leads.
2. **Visitor value:** give each visitor a useful estimate, diagnosis or recommendation.
3. **Mechanism:** paste one website page, compare two working lead magnets, publish one embed.
4. **Ongoing value:** understand leads, drop-off and the next action while Magnitai watches the
   live tool.
5. **Enabling technology:** AI generation, brand extraction, hosting and automatic protection.

AI explains how Magnitai works. It is not the reason a customer buys it.

The shortest accurate pitch is:

> Magnitai turns one of your website pages into a branded calculator, quiz or audit that gives
> visitors a useful result and gives your business the context to follow up.

## The complete product loop

`Website page → two working previews → account → review → purchase → publish → distribute → learn → improve`

Generation is the first-use experience. The repeat product is the
**learn → act → improve** loop.

## One screen, one job

| Screen | Its only primary job | Dominant action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Make the outcome understood and prove the product works. | Build from my website |
| Examples | Let a visitor experience the value exchange. | Play an example |
| How it works | Remove uncertainty about the journey and trust model. | Build from my website |
| Website entry | Collect the page that contains the offer. | Recommend my lead magnet |
| Guided brief | Preserve the few choices the owner cares about. | Create my lead magnets |
| Comparison | Choose between two complete visitor experiences. | Use this lead magnet |
| Review | Check questions, result and next action. | Build my lead magnet |
| Purchase | Choose one launch or an ongoing workspace. | Continue with the selected option |
| Tool detail | Resolve the next launch blocker or improve one part. | Contextual next action |
| Workspace | Explain the business outcome and recommend one action. | Next best action |

No screen should introduce a second product story. Technical capabilities belong where they
answer a trust question, not ahead of the customer outcome.

## Journey rules

### Value before commitment

- A visitor can submit a URL and try the output before creating an account.
- The account gate names the lead magnet and keeps it visible behind the dialog.
- The result appears before the contact request in the visitor experience.
- Nothing claims to be published, measured, repaired or protected until the backend confirms it.

### Familiar interaction

- Ask one question or decision at a time in the visitor experience.
- Prefer choices, sliders and bounded inputs over open text.
- Use calculator, quiz and audit in customer-facing language.
- Use one stable term—**lead magnet**—through marketing, builder, purchase and workspace.
- Never require the customer to understand agents, models, pipelines or generation stages.

### Advice needs evidence

The workspace recommendation order is:

1. Restore a failed tool.
2. Follow a build in progress.
3. Finish an unpublished tool.
4. Embed a live tool with no views.
5. Improve an opening only after at least 20 views and no starts.
6. Review questions only after at least 10 starts and under 50% completion.
7. Improve the result only after at least five completions and no leads.
8. Review leads when the funnel is producing them.
9. Otherwise, keep collecting a useful sample.

These are conservative product guardrails, not claimed industry benchmarks. They prevent
Magnitai from turning noise into advice.

### Trust must be evidenced

- Never invent customers, logos, testimonials, traffic or conversion claims.
- Show a real playable example when customer proof is not yet available.
- Use prices and entitlements from the live billing catalogue.
- Keep draft, building, live and failed states visibly distinct.
- Preserve the last safe live version when a replacement fails.
- Make lead answers, result and delivery history reachable from the lead record.

## Commercial readiness gates still to close

The core journey is implemented, but these are required before calling the product world-class:

1. **First-party acquisition telemetry:** record landing view, URL start, preview viewed, account
   created, purchase started, purchase completed, publish completed and first external impression.
2. **Production happy-path proof:** continuously verify one real URL through generation, account,
   payment, build, live embed, visitor completion, lead capture and owner follow-up.
3. **Distribution activation:** treat “published but never embedded” as incomplete activation and
   help the owner place and verify the embed.
4. **Credible customer proof:** add named case studies only after permission and measured results.
   Until then, playable product proof is the honest substitute.
5. **Operational trust:** publish clear support, security, data handling and incident-contact
   information that a business buyer can evaluate.
6. **Lead delivery reliability:** verify export and configured delivery paths, expose failures, and
   make retry or recovery obvious.

## Success measures

| Stage | Measure | Product question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Homepage → URL submitted | Did the visitor understand enough to act? |
| First value | URL submitted → preview completed | Did the result prove Magnitai understood the business? |
| Commitment | Preview completed → account created | Was the lead magnet worth keeping? |
| Purchase | Account created → purchase completed | Was the value and plan choice clear? |
| Activation | Purchase completed → first external impression | Did the owner actually place it on the site? |
| Visitor value | Start → result | Was the interaction worth completing? |
| Business value | Result → lead or final action | Did it create a useful commercial next step? |
| Retention | Owner returns and completes the recommendation | Does the workspace improve results over time? |
| Trust | Scheduled checks succeed and failures recover | Can a business rely on the live product? |
